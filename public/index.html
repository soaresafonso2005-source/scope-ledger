Questions

Do my clients need to do anything differently?

No. They keep using WhatsApp, email or Slack exactly as today. Only the account manager uses Scope Ledger, for a couple of minutes a week per client.

How do chats get into Scope Ledger?

From WhatsApp, export the client's chat and share it to Scope Ledger: on iPhone with a one-time Shortcut, on Android by installing the app from your browser. From Slack, use “Send to Scope Ledger” on any message. Everything lands in your team's inbox, matched to the right client. WhatsApp doesn't allow outside tools to read your groups directly, so nothing is read without you sharing it.

How is this different from asking ChatGPT?

A general chatbot gives you an opinion. Scope Ledger follows one method every time: it splits the request into separate asks, checks each against quantity, channel, format, revisions, timing and service, quotes your text as evidence and verifies those quotes, then gives you a reply and a change proposal in a fixed format your whole team can rely on.

Does it know what we've already delivered?

Yes. Each saved client has monthly allowances with a usage counter, plus the date the package ends. Every check uses those numbers, so a request that would go over the limit, or arrives after the contract ended, is flagged before you reply.

Do I need to upload my contract?

No. Paste the part of the proposal or package that describes what you deliver. The more specific it is about quantities, channels and revisions, the sharper the reading.

What if the request is vague?

Then Scope Ledger says so. It marks the request as ambiguous and gives you the exact questions to ask, instead of guessing.

Which AI do you use, and what happens to my text?

Analyses run on Anthropic's Claude models through their commercial API. Your text is sent once to produce the result and is not stored by Scope Ledger. See the privacy page.
