Send client chats to Scope Ledger

Your clients keep writing where they always do. Set this up once per phone or Slack workspace, and conversations land in your Scope Ledger inbox in two taps.

1. Your workspace

2. iPhone: share from WhatsApp

Create one Shortcut on each iPhone that will send chats. It takes about five minutes.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap + to create a new shortcut. Name it Send to Scope Ledger.
  2. Tap the ⓘ (Details) button and turn on Show in Share Sheet. In the first block, “Receive Any input from Share Sheet”, set the types to Text and Files.
  3. Add the action Get Contents of URL. Paste this address as the URL:
    Sign in first to see your address
  4. Tap the arrow to show more options. Set Method to POST and Request Body to Form. Add a field of type File, with key file and value Shortcut Input.
  5. Add the action Show Notification and choose Contents of URL as the text. Tap Done.

Using it

In WhatsApp, open the client's chat or group, tap its name, then Export Chat › Without Media, and choose Send to Scope Ledger in the share sheet. You'll see “Sent to your Scope Ledger inbox”. You can also select any text, tap Share and choose the same shortcut.

3. Android: share from WhatsApp

  1. Open this site/app.html in Chrome and sign in to your workspace.
  2. Open Chrome's menu (⋮) and tap Add to Home screen or Install app. Open Scope Ledger once from your home screen.
  3. In WhatsApp, open the client's chat, tap ⋮ › More › Export chat › Without media, and choose Scope Ledger. The chat opens in the app, matched to the right client, ready to scan.
